# Project AlphaFold — Technical Summary

AlphaFold is a protein structure prediction system developed by DeepMind,
the London-based AI lab led by Demis Hassabis. The system predicts 3D
structures of proteins from their amino acid sequences.

John Jumper led the AlphaFold 2 team. The work was published in Nature in
2021. DeepMind released the AlphaFold protein structure database publicly.

## Preferences

The team prefers open release of scientific software and datasets. They
prefer permissive licenses (Apache 2.0, CC-BY) over restrictive terms.

# Demis Hassabis — Biography

Demis Hassabis is a British artificial intelligence researcher and
entrepreneur. He co-founded DeepMind in 2010 and served as its CEO until
2024 when Google merged DeepMind with Google Brain to form Google DeepMind.

Hassabis was born in London in 1976. He is a chess prodigy and former child
actor. He studied at Cambridge University, earning a double first in
computer science.

DeepMind is headquartered in London, United Kingdom. The company is best
known for AlphaGo, AlphaFold, and advances in reinforcement learning.
